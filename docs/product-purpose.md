# Product purpose

Issue #9. Business record.

Park the Bus is a free daily soccer trivia game from Truepeak Software LLC, in this repository. It is for soccer fans who like trivia. Everyone plays the same prompts each day, many answers are valid, and a rarer correct answer scores more. The aim is interesting trivia on a screen that is easy to use. The MVP charges nothing. A later paid tier may unlock more quizzes and specific modes. That tier is not part of the MVP, and payments stay out of scope until a later decision.
